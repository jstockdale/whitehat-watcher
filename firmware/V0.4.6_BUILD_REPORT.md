## Changes

### 1. WDT: active watchdog with HAL feeding (S3 RWDT)

**Before:** `CONFIG_BOOTLOADER_WDT_DISABLE_IN_USER_CODE` was removed, intending
to keep the WDT active. This was **wrong**: ESP-IDF's `init_disable_rtc_wdt()`
startup hook then *disabled* the RTC WDT — the opposite of the goal. Additionally,
`rtc_wdt_feed()` does not exist on ESP32-S3 (guarded `#if ESP32 || ESP32S2`).

**After:**
- `CONFIG_BOOTLOADER_WDT_DISABLE_IN_USER_CODE=y` (keeps bootloader RWDT armed into the app).
- `main.c` feeds the S3 RWDT via `wdt_hal_feed()` every 1s in the supervisor loop.
- Task WDT 5s + panic, INT WDT 300ms unchanged.

**Impact:** If the main loop hangs, the RTC WDT WILL fire (reset reason 7).
The safety net works instead of being silently disabled.

### 2. RAM: 8 tasks moved to PSRAM stacks

**Before:** 8 tasks used internal DRAM stacks: snap, snaptest, hid_keepalive,
input, input_retry, link_rx, mqtt_native, ww_feed.

**After:** All 8 use `task_psram_create()` with stacks in PSRAM.
- `nvs_writer` stays internal (NVS/flash constraint).
- `task_psram` now tracks task handles; transient tasks use deferred cleanup
  (no leaks on snap/snaptest/input_retry/ww_feed self-delete).

**Correction:** The RAM audit claimed stack sizes were in "words" (4096 words =
16KB). ESP-IDF `xTaskCreate` stack depth is in **bytes**. The audit's ~168KB
recovery estimate was 4x overstated; actual is ~42KB from stacks alone.

### 3. RAM: transient heap buffers to PSRAM

**Before:** eyes snap/snaptest jobs, link JSON/command buffers, ptt base64,
mqtt_native MQTT/JSON buffers used internal heap.

**After:** All moved to `heap_caps_malloc(MALLOC_CAP_SPIRAM)`.
- `wms2.c` scratch intentionally left as-is (must stay pure C99 for host gcc tests).
