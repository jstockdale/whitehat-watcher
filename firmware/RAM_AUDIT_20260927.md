# Watcher Firmware Internal-RAM Audit — 2026-09-27

**Scope:** Full audit of internal-DRAM (0x3FC88000–0x3FD00000) consumption.
**Constraint honored:** Recommendations only. No source files modified.
**Baseline (`internal_free` from device heartbeats):** 0.3.11: 13,610 B → 0.4.0: 7,142 B → 0.4.1: 5,590 B → 0.4.2: 1,546 B.

## Method

Static analysis of `build/watcher_input_poc.elf` (`xtensa-esp32s3-elf-nm --size-sort`),
`sbcconfig`/`sdkconfig`, and source-level allocation audit across all components.
Total `.bss` in internal DRAM: **69.7 KB** (static). Remainder is heap-allocated
at runtime (task stacks, WiFi/BT drivers, malloc).

## Where the RAM went (0.3.11 → 0.4.2 delta, −12 KB)

| Version step | Δ free | Attributed cause |
|---|---|---|
| 0.3.11 → 0.4.0 | −6.5 KB | Seal/WIA1/native-MQTT additions (see below) |
| 0.4.0 → 0.4.1 | −1.5 KB | `set_name`, `mqtt_broker`/`mqtt_topic`, NVS `dev` namespace |
| 0.4.1 → 0.4.2 | −4.0 KB | Persistent 4 KB DMA staging buffer (sscma_client_io_spi.c:148) |

**Seal/WIA1/native-MQTT static cost (the 0.4.0 delta), all internal DRAM:**
- `mqtt_native` task stack: **6,144 B** (mqtt_native.c:652, `xTaskCreate`, always created at boot via link_init → mqtt_native_init, even though native MQTT is dormant)
- `seal_nvs` writer task stack: **4,096 B** (seal_dev.c:335 — required internal, do not move)
- `in_env_`: **2,152 B** (mqtt_native.c:71 — sealed envelope RX buffer, `.bss`)
- `fwd_env_`: **2,152 B** (link.c:735 — sealed_fwd verify buffer, function-static `.bss`)
- `verify_scratch_`: **2,218 B** (seal_dev.c:83 — WMS2 verify scratch, `.bss`)
- Queues (`in_q_`, `tx_q_`): small
- WIA1 session structs, `cmd_pk_`/`tel_seed_` (32 B each): small
- **Seal subtotal: ~17 KB** of the −6.5 KB net delta (remainder absorbed by heap layout shifts; net free-heap delta understates gross consumption because some 0.3.11 allocations moved)

## Recommendations (ranked by saving × safety)

### R1. Defer WiFi init until credentials are provisioned — ~35 KB, MEDIUM risk
**What:** `net_init()` (net.c:86–109) calls `esp_wifi_init()` + `esp_wifi_start()`
unconditionally at boot. Native MQTT is dormant (bridged mode); with no saved
credentials the WiFi stack burns ~35–45 KB internal for nothing.
**Change:** Do not init/start WiFi at boot when no credentials are stored.
Init on first `wifi_cred`/`wifi` provisioning instead; deinit if credentials are cleared.
**Risk:** Medium — behavioral change. Display WiFi indicator, `net_event_cb`,
and the `wifi`/`wifi_cred`/`mqtt_broker` flows must handle "WiFi not initialized"
gracefully. Reconnect logic must trigger init.
**Needs John's decision:** Yes — this changes boot behavior.

### R2. Move seal crypto buffers to PSRAM — 6.4 KB, LOW risk
**What:** `in_env_` (mqtt_native.c:71), `fwd_env_` (link.c:735), `verify_scratch_`
(seal_dev.c:83) are `.bss` in internal DRAM. They hold base64-decoded envelopes
and signature-verify scratch — CPU-only, no DMA, no ISR, no cache-disable interaction.
**Change:** `EXT_RAM_BSS_ATTR` (or SPIRAM `heap_caps_malloc` at init).
**Risk:** Low — slight PSRAM latency on the verify path (per-command, not hot).
No behavior change. Security posture unchanged (RAM is RAM).
**Saving:** 2,152 + 2,152 + 2,218 = **6,522 B.**

### R3. Lower `SPIRAM_MALLOC_ALWAYSINTERNAL` 16384 → 4096 — est. 2–5 KB, MEDIUM risk
**What:** sdkconfig sets `CONFIG_SPIRAM_MALLOC_ALWAYSINTERNAL=16384`: every plain
`malloc()` under 16 KB goes to internal DRAM. The seal/mqtt_native/link code
uses plain `malloc()` for message buffers, JSON strings, base64 (~2 KB), etc.
**Change:** Lower to 4096. Allocations ≥4 KB go to PSRAM; small ones stay internal.
**Risk:** Medium — some IDF components use plain `malloc()` for buffers that
secretly require internal (DMA-adjacent, ISR). Must audit all plain-malloc sites
for DMA/ISR use before changing. The 0.4.3 build-time PSRAM/flash audit is the
right vehicle to extend.
**Saving:** Estimated 2–5 KB (dozens of small mallocs across seal/link/mqtt paths).

### R4. Investigate GTCRN tables (16.4 KB) — HIGH saving, MEDIUM-HIGH risk
**What:** `gtcrn_tanh_tbl` + `gtcrn_sig_tbl` (8,208 B each) from the ESP-SR AFE,
in internal `.bss`. Pulled in by noise suppression (`ns_init=true`, wakeword.c:221).
AFE already uses `AFE_MEMORY_ALLOC_MORE_PSRAM`, but these tables are static in the
precompiled lib.
**Change (to investigate):** Does `ns_init=false` (and/or `se_init=false`) drop
the tables from the link (GC sections)? If yes, measure wakeword accuracy impact.
**Risk:** Medium-high — directly affects wakeword/noise-suppression quality.
**Needs John's decision:** Yes — accuracy vs. RAM tradeoff. Do not change blindly.

### R5. Right-size task stacks via high-water measurement — est. 4–8 KB, LOW risk
**What:** Several 4 KB internal stacks look generous: `hid_keepalive`, `input`,
`seal_nvs`, `snap`/`snaptest` (transient). `mqtt_native` at 6 KB is the largest.
**Change:** Measure `uxTaskGetStackHighWaterMark()` for each task under load,
then trim to high-water + 1 KB guard. Candidates: `mqtt_native` 6144→4096,
`hid_keepalive` 4096→2048, `input` 4096→2048.
**Risk:** Low *if* measured under worst-case load (TLS handshake for mqtt_native,
burst input for input task). Never trim without measurement.
**Saving:** 4–8 KB depending on measurements.

### R6. Defer `mqtt_native` task until provisioned — 6 KB + queues, LOW risk
**What:** `mqtt_native_init()` runs at every boot (link.c:1912), creating the
6,144 B task + queues, even though native MQTT never connects without
provisioned WiFi/MQTT credentials.
**Change:** Create the task/queues lazily on first native-MQTT provisioning
(or as part of R1's WiFi bring-up).
**Risk:** Low — the task already handles "not provisioned" by idling; deferral
just moves creation later. Pairs naturally with R1.
**Saving:** ~6.5 KB.

### R7. Trim WiFi static buffers (if R1 deferred) — est. 12–19 KB, LOW-MEDIUM risk
**What:** If WiFi must stay initialized at boot, reduce
`CONFIG_ESP_WIFI_STATIC_RX_BUFFER_NUM` 8→4 and
`CONFIG_ESP_WIFI_STATIC_TX_BUFFER_NUM` 16→8.
**Change:** sdkconfig only.
**Risk:** Low-medium — reduces WiFi throughput/robustness under load. Fine for
MQTT telemetry (small, infrequent); risky if bulk transfer over native MQTT
is planned.
**Saving:** ~19 KB (8×1600 + 8×1600). Half-step (12→8 TX): ~6.4 KB.

### R8. Shrink LWIP DNS table — 0.6–1 KB, LOW risk
**What:** `dns_table` 1,184 B (default `DNS_TABLE_SIZE=2` entries? — verify).
**Change:** Reduce if only one concurrent DNS lookup is ever needed.
**Risk:** Low. **Saving:** <1 KB. Only worth it as a bundle with other small wins.

### R9. Audit `line$3` (4 KB) and `pcm$3` (2 KB) compiler statics — est. 2–6 KB, LOW risk
**What:** Two large function-static buffers. Identify the functions; if they are
cold-path (log formatting, one-shot PCM), move to PSRAM or shrink.
**Risk:** Low once identified. **Saving:** TBD by identification.

## Fragmentation note

At 1,546 B free, the *largest free block* matters more than the total. The
0.4.2 DMA staging buffer is allocated once at boot (no fragmentation from it),
but the WiFi driver's dynamic buffers and per-operation `malloc()`s
(seal envelope handling, cJSON in the link path, `fill_rows` transient DMA
chunks in display.c:539) fragment the remainder. Any fix that only raises the
total without addressing the largest-block floor (currently likely <1 KB)
will not survive contact with `nvs_commit` or a TLS handshake. Recommend
logging `heap_caps_get_largest_free_block(MALLOC_CAP_INTERNAL)` in the
heartbeat alongside `internal_free` (firmware change, trivial, high diagnostic value).

## Per-operation internal allocs (danger spots — all currently unfenced)

- `display.c:539` `fill_rows()`: transient `MALLOC_CAP_DMA` up to
  `LCD_HRES × 64 × 2` B per call — fails silently (`ESP_ERR_NO_MEM` → caller handles?)
- `ptt.c:406`: `malloc(2049)` base64 per chunk — internal by threshold (R3 covers)
- `mqtt_native.c` message/JSON mallocs — internal by threshold (R3 covers)
- `wms2.c:128` verify scratch `malloc()` — internal by threshold (R3 covers)
- cJSON parse/print in link worker — internal by threshold (R3 covers); worker is PSRAM-stack so a large cJSON doc risks stack, not heap
