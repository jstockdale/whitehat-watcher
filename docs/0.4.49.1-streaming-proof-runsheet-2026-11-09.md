# 0.4.49.1 streaming-audio proof — bench sheet (2026-11-09)

Purpose: close the last open verification item from the 0.4.49 streaming
rework (design: `docs/audio-streaming-design-2026-11-09.md`, verification
plan items 2–5). The firmware is already flashed and proven on its other
fronts; what has never had John's voice on it is the streaming shipper
itself. Nothing here authorizes a flash or a device command — each step
waits on John's go in the bench session.

## State going in (verified, do not re-run)

- **fw 0.4.49 flashed and heartbeat-verified 2026-11-09** — `fw-flashed
  8dc474bd`, heartbeats healthy (heap ~3.03 MB), wake-word listening, mic
  reads climbing clean (4,470 reads, err=0), no panics on the new boot.
  0.4.49 carries both the streaming shipper and the I2C fix.
- **fw 0.4.49.1 flashed and verified the same day** — the only change
  heap-allocates the `dbg png` context in PSRAM (mirroring the snaptest
  fix from v0.4.18.64) plus temporary timer-list probes. The `dbg png`
  battery passed **3-for-3 clean** (1,807,824 B, 224 chunks, ok:true, no
  panic) vs 2-for-2 fatal on 0.4.49. The audio path is untouched by
  0.4.49.1, so the streaming proof runs against 0.4.49.1 as-is.
- **I2C interrupt-WDT is root-caused and fixed** — the vendor PCA9535
  driver never engages its read cache without an interrupt pin, so the
  10 ms wheel poll issued a real I2C0 transaction ~100×/s; the fix
  (sample ≤ every 50 ms, 250 ms backoff on failure, I2C0 400→100 kHz)
  shipped in 0.4.49. The earlier investigation brief is background now,
  not an open hunt.
- **Crop battery passed end to end** (1296×972 twice, 2592×486 strip)
  and the README pass it gated is pushed. Not part of this sheet.

## Preflight — in John's standing order

1. **Bridge health first:** `bridge_health.py` exit 0 (subscriber alive,
   telemetry fresh). No bench traffic before this passes.
2. **Bench lock held** for the session (`bench_lock.py`).
3. **Seal floor resync** from freshest telemetry before the first send.
   Caution from 2026-11-09: after a crash-reboot the device floor jumped
   to 77571 while the telemetry-derived floor still read 77070, and two
   sealed sends were silently dropped as REPLAY until a manual resync.
   The device has also been power-cycled/rebooted several times during
   the evening link-wedge work — resync at session start, and re-check
   after any reboot before blaming a command path.
4. **Record the firmware from the heartbeat** (expect fw=0.4.49.1) —
   never assume it.

## The proof — gated sequence (each step on John's go)

1. **Wake-word streaming take.** "Hey Muse" + a few seconds of speech.
   Pass bar, all of:
   - audio chunks arrive at the bridge *during* the take (streaming,
     ~5 msg/s trickle per the design), not as one terminal burst;
   - the take transcribes correctly;
   - `audio_end` stats sane, `dropped` absent, chunk count consistent
     with take length.
2. **Sub-300 ms tap — the discard test.** One quick button tap.
   Pass bar: **zero audio traffic** in telemetry (the commit point holds
   everything below PTT_MIN_MS in the ring and discards it).
3. **Back-to-back takes — re-arm test.** A second wake-word take
   immediately after the first completes. Pass bar: the wake word
   detects again on the same boot (the 0.4.45 re-arm fix holding under
   the new sender-task completion sequence), the second take streams
   and transcribes, and `take_done` gating shows no interleave.
4. **Long take (~30 s, to the cap).** Pass bar: streams throughout,
   ships complete at the cap, `audio_end` stop reason is the cap,
   no ring-overflow drop count.

Any step failing names the next hunt; all four passing closes the
streaming rework, and 0.4.49.1's source becomes eligible for the next
clean-copy push (it is not in the pushed history yet — clean copy is
at 0.4.49 / commit 6164b05).
